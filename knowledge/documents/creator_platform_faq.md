# Creator Platform FAQ (example knowledge doc)

## Payout schedule
Creators are paid out on a monthly basis, on the 1st business day of each month, for the previous month's earnings. Minimum payout threshold is $20.

## Content ownership
Creators retain full ownership of the content they upload. The platform receives a limited, non-exclusive license to host and display the content to paying subscribers.

## Subscription pricing
Creators can set their own monthly subscription price between $4.99 and $49.99. Platform fee is 20% of gross revenue.

## Verification requirements
All creators must complete identity verification, including a government-issued ID check and a liveness check, before publishing any content. This is a platform-wide, non-negotiable requirement.

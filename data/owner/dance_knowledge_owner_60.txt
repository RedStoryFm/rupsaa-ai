1) Belly Dance
Origin: Middle East / North Africa (Egypt, Turkey, Lebanon, etc.).
Description: It is an expressive, traditional dance form that focuses on fluid, rhythmic isolations and movements of the torso and hips.

2) Ballet
Origin: Italy, later developed in France and Russia.
Description: It is a highly technical classical dance form known for pointe work, turn-out, grace, and storytelling.

3) Tap Dance
Origin: United States.
Description: It is a rhythmic dance form where dancers wear metal-tipped shoes and create percussion sounds with their feet.

4) Jazz Dance
Origin: United States.
Description: It is an energetic dance style blending ballet technique, isolations, syncopation, and theatrical performance.

5) Hip-Hop
Origin: United States (Bronx, New York).
Description: It is a street dance style tied to hip-hop culture, featuring grooves, freestyle, battles, and strong musicality.

6) Breaking / Breakdance
Origin: United States (South Bronx, New York).
Description: It is an athletic street dance form with top rocks, footwork, freezes, and power moves, danced to breakbeats.

7) Popping
Origin: United States (California).
Description: It is a funk street dance style based on quickly contracting and relaxing muscles to create a “pop” or hit.

8) Locking
Origin: United States (Los Angeles).
Description: It is a funk dance style with sharp arm movements, holds, points, and playful character.

9) Krump
Origin: United States (Los Angeles).
Description: It is a high-energy street dance style using aggressive, expressive movements, chest pops, stomps, and freestyle expression.

10) House Dance
Origin: United States (Chicago and New York).
Description: It is a club dance style with fast footwork, jacking, lofting, and fluid floor movements, danced to house music.

11) Waacking
Origin: United States (Los Angeles, LGBTQ+ club scene).
Description: It is a disco-era dance style known for dramatic arm movements, poses, and attitude.

12) Voguing
Origin: United States (New York, Harlem ballroom scene).
Description: It is a stylized dance form with model-like poses, lines, angles, and categories such as Old Way, New Way, and Vogue Fem.

13) Dancehall
Origin: Jamaica.
Description: It is an energetic social dance style performed to dancehall reggae, known for hip movements, attitude, and improvisation.

14) Reggaeton
Origin: Puerto Rico / Panama / Caribbean.
Description: It is a club dance style performed to reggaeton music, blending hip movements, Latin rhythms, and urban street style.

15) Salsa
Origin: Cuba / Puerto Rico / New York.
Description: It is a lively partner dance with syncopated steps, turns, and hip motion, danced to salsa music.

16) Bachata
Origin: Dominican Republic.
Description: It is a romantic partner dance with a four-step rhythm, close embrace, and gentle hip movement.

17) Merengue
Origin: Dominican Republic.
Description: It is a fast, simple partner dance with a marching step and strong hip action.

18) Cha-Cha
Origin: Cuba.
Description: It is a playful Latin ballroom dance with a triple step and rhythmic hip motion.

19) Rumba
Origin: Cuba.
Description: It is a slow, expressive Latin dance focused on hip movement, connection, and romantic storytelling.

20) Mambo
Origin: Cuba / New York.
Description: It is a Latin dance with strong syncopation, quick steps, and energetic turns.

21) Samba
Origin: Brazil.
Description: It is a lively Brazilian dance with rapid footwork, hip action, and Carnival-style energy.

22) Tango
Origin: Argentina / Uruguay.
Description: It is a dramatic partner dance known for close embrace, sharp leg movements, and intense musicality.

23) Milonga
Origin: Argentina / Uruguay.
Description: It is a faster, more relaxed cousin of tango with a steady rhythm and playful steps.

24) Cumbia
Origin: Colombia.
Description: It is a folkloric and social dance with shuffling steps, hip movement, and circular partner patterns.

25) Forró
Origin: Brazil.
Description: It is a Brazilian partner dance with close embrace, simple steps, and rhythmic turns.

26) Flamenco
Origin: Spain (Andalusia).
Description: It is a passionate dance form with percussive footwork, hand clapping, guitar music, and expressive arm movements.

27) Sevillanas
Origin: Spain (Seville).
Description: It is a festive Spanish folk dance with patterned footwork, castanets, and partner turns.

28) Irish Step Dance
Origin: Ireland.
Description: It is a traditional dance style with rigid upper body, quick footwork, and reels, jigs, and hornpipes.

29) Highland Fling
Origin: Scotland.
Description: It is a traditional Scottish solo dance with precise footwork, jumps, and arms held in position.

30) Morris Dance
Origin: England.
Description: It is a traditional English folk dance with rhythmic stepping, bells, sticks, and handkerchiefs.

31) Polka
Origin: Czech Republic / Bohemia.
Description: It is a fast, lively partner dance with hopping steps and a cheerful 2/4 rhythm.

32) Mazurka
Origin: Poland.
Description: It is a traditional Polish dance in triple time with lively turns, heel clicks, and folk character.

33) Tarantella
Origin: Italy (Southern Italy).
Description: It is a fast, whirling folk dance often accompanied by tambourines and lively music.

34) Waltz
Origin: Austria / Germany.
Description: It is a smooth ballroom dance in 3/4 time with flowing turns and rise-and-fall movement.

35) Foxtrot
Origin: United States.
Description: It is a smooth ballroom dance with long, walking steps and a relaxed, jazzy feel.

36) Quickstep
Origin: England / United States.
Description: It is a fast ballroom dance with quick steps, runs, hops, and lively turns.

37) Bharatanatyam
Origin: India (Tamil Nadu).
Description: It is a classical Indian dance with geometric poses, expressive gestures, rhythmic footwork, and storytelling.

38) Kathak
Origin: India (North India).
Description: It is a classical Indian dance known for spins, intricate footwork, and expressive storytelling.

39) Kathakali
Origin: India (Kerala).
Description: It is a classical dance-drama with elaborate makeup, costumes, and codified gestures.

40) Kuchipudi
Origin: India (Andhra Pradesh).
Description: It is a classical Indian dance combining graceful movement, rhythmic footwork, and dramatic expression.

41) Odissi
Origin: India (Odisha).
Description: It is a classical Indian dance with sculptural poses, fluid torso movement, and devotional themes.

42) Bhangra
Origin: India / Pakistan (Punjab).
Description: It is an energetic folk dance with strong shoulder and hip movements, often performed at celebrations.

43) Garba
Origin: India (Gujarat).
Description: It is a circular devotional folk dance performed with claps and graceful turns, often during Navratri.

44) Lavani
Origin: India (Maharashtra).
Description: It is a traditional dance-drama form with rhythmic footwork, expressive gestures, and lively music.

45) Lion Dance
Origin: China.
Description: It is a traditional dance performed with a lion costume, drumming, and martial-arts-like movements for luck and celebration.

46) Dragon Dance
Origin: China.
Description: It is a festive dance where performers move a long dragon puppet through coordinated waves and turns.

47) Kabuki
Origin: Japan.
Description: It is a classical Japanese dance-drama with stylized movement, elaborate costumes, and dramatic makeup.

48) Butoh
Origin: Japan.
Description: It is an avant-garde dance form with slow, controlled, and often surreal or grotesque movements.

49) Kecak
Origin: Indonesia (Bali).
Description: It is a dramatic Balinese dance performance with a large male chorus chanting “cak” in interlocking rhythms.

50) Hula
Origin: Hawaii.
Description: It is a traditional Hawaiian dance with flowing gestures, hip movements, and chants or songs that tell stories.

51) Haka
Origin: New Zealand (Māori).
Description: It is a powerful group dance with stomping, chanting, and fierce facial expressions, performed for welcome, war, or celebration.

52) Dabke
Origin: Levant (Lebanon, Syria, Palestine, Jordan, Iraq).
Description: It is a line or circle folk dance with stomping, linked arms, and communal celebration.

53) Tanoura
Origin: Egypt.
Description: It is a Sufi folk dance where a male dancer spins continuously in a colorful skirt, often with LED lights.

54) Sufi Whirling
Origin: Turkey (Mevlevi Sufi tradition).
Description: It is a meditative spinning dance performed to achieve spiritual ecstasy and connection with the divine.

55) Kizomba
Origin: Angola.
Description: It is a sensual partner dance with close embrace, slow rhythmic steps, and African and Latin influences.

56) Zouk
Origin: French Caribbean (Guadeloupe, Martinique).
Description: It is a smooth partner dance with flowing turns, close connection, and rhythmic hip movement.

57) Azonto
Origin: Ghana.
Description: It is a fast, expressive street dance with hand gestures, knee movements, and everyday-life storytelling.

58) Eskista
Origin: Ethiopia.
Description: It is a traditional dance known for intense shoulder shimmying, bouncing, and rhythmic isolation.

59) Gumboot
Origin: South Africa.
Description: It is a rhythmic dance created by mine workers, performed with gumboots and body percussion.

60) Lindy Hop
Origin: United States (Harlem, New York).
Description: It is a swing partner dance with energetic footwork, aerial moves, and improvisation.
